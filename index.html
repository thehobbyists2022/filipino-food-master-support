Frequently asked questions

Do I need an account?

No. Every feature is available without signing in.

Does the app work offline?

Yes. Core recipes and tools are stored locally on your device and work without a connection.

Are the recipes safe for allergies?

Recipes include common allergens such as fish sauce, shrimp paste, peanuts and gluten, and include substitution notes. Always check packaged ingredient labels and follow safe cooking temperatures.

How do I reset my preferences?

Clear the app data in your device settings, or reinstall the app. Your saved preferences are stored only on your device.

Do the ingredient links cost me anything?

Some links are affiliate links. If you choose to buy through them, we may earn a small commission at no extra cost to you. All guides remain fully usable without them.
