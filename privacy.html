Privacy Policy & Food Safety Terms

Last updated: September 13, 2026

Summary: Filipino Food Master is a privacy-first cooking companion. We do not collect personal data, do not require registration, and do not track your location. Your preferences (language, units, cookware mode, favorites) are stored only on your device.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

  • No Account Required: All features (recipes, step timers, cookware modes, phrase cards, Halo-Halo Lab) work without creating an account.
  • No Sensitive Personal Data: We do not collect or store your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, or camera feed.
  • No Payments: We do not process financial transactions or store payment details inside the app.
  • No Location Tracking: The app does not request or use your location.

2. Local Storage & Device Preferences

To support seamless offline cooking, Filipino Food Master uses local storage strictly on your device to remember:

  • Selected language (English, Traditional Chinese, Filipino, Japanese, Korean)
  • Measurement unit preference (Metric or US Standard)
  • Cookware mode selection (Traditional, Air Fryer, Pressure Cooker)
  • Favorited recipes and personal notes
  • Audio timer settings

This data never leaves your device and can be cleared at any time through your device settings or by reinstalling the app.

3. Affiliate & Retailer Links

The app may provide optional ingredient sourcing links:

  • Affiliate Disclosure: Some links are affiliate links. If you choose to make a purchase through them, we may earn a small commission at no additional cost to you. This does not affect the price you pay.
  • No Tracking or Ad Networks: We do not use behavior-tracking scripts, invasive third-party ad networks, or deceptive referral schemes. All recipe content is available without using any link.

4. Food Safety & Cooking Disclaimer

Kitchen Safety Notice: Authentic Filipino cooking can involve high-heat frying, sizzling plates, and pressure cooking.
  • High-Heat & Splatter: Deep-frying dishes such as Crispy Pata, Lumpia, Fish Balls and Turon requires care. Dry ingredients thoroughly before frying and use a splatter screen.
  • Safe Internal Temperatures: Ensure poultry reaches at least 165°F / 74°C and pork reaches at least 145°F / 63°C.
  • Allergies: Filipino cuisine commonly uses fish sauce, shrimp paste, peanuts, and gluten. Always verify packaged ingredients and use the substitution notes when cooking for people with allergies.
  • Educational Purpose: The app provides general cooking guidance only and is not professional, medical, or nutritional advice. You assume full responsibility for kitchen safety, appliance operation, and personal food tolerance.

5. Children's Privacy (COPPA)

The app does not knowingly collect any personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13. It is intended for a general audience and family cooking education.

6. Your Rights

Because we do not collect personal data, there is no personal data for us to access, correct, export, or delete. You remain in full control of the information stored on your device.
